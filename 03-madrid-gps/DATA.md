# Data

## cruces.csv (included)

Street intersections of Madrid with coordinates: "Callejero. Información adicional asociada. Cruces de viales con coordenadas geográficas", CSV, from the Madrid open data portal.

- Publisher: Ayuntamiento de Madrid, Portal de datos abiertos.
- Dataset page: https://datos.madrid.es/dataset/200075-0-callejero
- Terms of use: https://datos.madrid.es/pages/condiciones-de-uso , which state that the portal's data is reusable for commercial and non-commercial purposes under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- Downloaded in January 2024 (file date 2024-01-08). The portal updates this file, so a new download can differ from this copy.
- The file is used unchanged: semicolon separated, 8-bit Latin-1 text (not UTF-8), 36,165 data rows and 14 columns.

Attribution: Ayuntamiento de Madrid, Portal de datos abiertos, "Callejero. Información adicional asociada. Cruces de viales con coordenadas geográficas", CC BY 4.0.

## direcciones.csv (not included)

"Direcciones postales vigentes con coordenadas geográficas" from the same dataset, about 95 MB. It is left out because of its size. Download the CSV from the dataset page above, save it as `direcciones.csv` next to `gps.py` and keep the semicolon-separated format. The program was written and run against the January 2024 edition.
